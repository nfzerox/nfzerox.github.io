Virtual Mac on iPad v1.2.2

People have dreamed of running macOS on iPad for more than a decade. Today, that dream comes true. With Virtual Mac, iPad finally breaks free from iPadOS, enabling pro apps like Xcode, Terminal, Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, and Pixelmator Pro to run directly on device. Requires iPad Pro (M1, M2) or iPad Air (M1) running iPadOS 14 up to 16.3.1, or iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 14 Pro Max running iOS 16 up to 16.3.1.

TLSFix v1.1

Modern SSL/TLS for legacy iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch running iPhoneOS 2 to iOS 9. Reroutes the device's TLS/SSL through OpenSSL 1.1.1 (TLS 1.0 through 1.3, modern ciphers like ChaCha20-Poly1305 and AES-GCM) so old Safari and apps can reach today's servers again. Certificates are verified against the device's own system trust store, and SSL pinning and mutual TLS keep working.

Download .deb · Source & docs
